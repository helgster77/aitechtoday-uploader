AI & Tech Today Uploader

AI & Tech Today Uploader is a private, internal tool used by the owner of the AI & Tech Today YouTube channel. It uploads videos that the channel produces to that one channel, using the YouTube Data API.

For each video it sets the title, description, tags, chapters, thumbnail, audience setting and the altered or synthetic content disclosure. It has a single user, the channel owner, and is not offered to anyone else.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
